Questions owners ask

Do I have to stop using the software I already have?
No. If you already use a booking app, accounting software or a delivery service, I look for a way to work with it first. I only suggest replacing something when it is clearly causing problems.
How much does a website cost?
Websites start at $1,200 for a one-page site. A five-page site is $2,500 and a full site with photography is $3,800. You can pay in two halves or spread it over 12 months. Business tools are quoted after a consultation. See all prices.
Do you only work with businesses on Long Island?
I'm based near Hicksville and meet local clients in person. Businesses anywhere can work with me by video and phone.
Who owns the website?
You do. Your domain stays in your name, and the finished site is yours once it is paid in full.
Can my clients send me sensitive documents through the site?
Not through an ordinary contact form. Tax returns, IDs and similar files need a proper secure portal with access controls. If you need one, we plan it together, often by connecting a portal you already use.
What happens after the site launches?
You can choose a monthly care plan, and I keep the site online, backed up and up to date. Or skip the plan and pay by the hour when you need a change.
¿Hablas español?
Sí. Podemos reunirnos y trabajar en español.
