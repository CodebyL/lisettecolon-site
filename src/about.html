Why I work this way

I help run day-to-day operations at my family's property management business: tenants, vendors, repairs, showings and rent records. Most of a small business's day is phone calls, paperwork and remembering to follow up. A good website helps with all of that, not only with looking professional.

Before that I worked in IT support, setting up accounts and devices and training staff on new software, and in retail management, where I scheduled and supervised teams of about 30 people. Both taught me to explain technology in plain language and to build things people actually use.

I also take photos and fly a drone for my clients' sites, so the pictures show your real work.
