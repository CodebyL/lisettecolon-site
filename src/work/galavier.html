The business

Galavier Group is my family's real estate investment and property management company in New York City and Long Island. I also work there, helping with tenants, vendors, repairs, showings and records, so I build for problems I see every day.

What it needed

A professional website for prospective tenants and property owners, and a way to take rental applications, maintenance requests and documents without everything running through phone calls and email.

What I did

I planned, designed, wrote and coded the whole site, and I keep updating it. It is live today.

What it includes

  • Rental listings with photos, floor plans and availability
  • Online rental applications with status tracking
  • Tenant portal: lease, balance, payment history, documents
  • Maintenance requests with photos
  • Owner portal and owner services pages
  • Admin settings for social and messaging links
  • News and project updates
  • Contact form, team page and FAQ

What it shows

The same pieces work for other businesses: a public site that answers questions, a private sign-in where each customer sees only their own records, and an admin side where the owner keeps it current. No tenant information is shown here.
