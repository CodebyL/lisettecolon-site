The business

Scanline Aerials photographs properties and documents roofs from the air for developers, sellers and agents, homeowners, inspectors, insurance adjusters and contractors across Long Island, Queens, Brooklyn, the Bronx and Westchester.

What it needed

Very different customers needed to see quickly which service fits them, what it starts at, and how to ask for a quote with the property details filled in.

What I did

I designed and built the entire website: layout, copy structure, interactive pieces, area pages and the owner login.

What it includes

  • Quote form that asks who you are, the property address and a preferred visit time
  • Packages with starting prices
  • A "hold to scan" roof demo
  • A hero video that plays as you scroll
  • Project gallery with a full-screen viewer
  • A sample single-property listing site
  • Pages for each service area
  • FAQ and an owner login
