Pay an invoice

Every invoice arrives by email with a secure Pay button. Use that button to pay by card or bank account. You can also sign in to your client account to see all of your invoices and receipts.

Ways to pay

  • Card or bankThrough the secure link on your invoice, handled by Stripe. I never see or store your card number.
  • Zelle, cash, checkWelcome for one-time payments. Ask me for details; I'll record the payment and send you a receipt.
  • Monthly plansCharged automatically to a saved card or bank account. You can update it from your client account.
  • QuestionsEmail hi@lisettecolon.com with your invoice number.

For your safety, I will never ask for your card number by email, text or phone. If you get a message asking for payment details that doesn't link to an invoice from me, contact me first. Payment and cancellation terms
