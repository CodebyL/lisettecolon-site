Zarin's Delights

A website for a home bakery on Long Island that makes halal, alcohol-free custom desserts, built so customers can see the menu and prices, build an order and hold their date without a long back-and-forth in DMs.

Visit zarinsdelights.com
zarinsdelights.com

The business

Zarin bakes cupcakes, cake pops, chocolate-covered strawberries, tres leches and full dessert tables to order for birthdays, showers and holidays. Every dessert is halal and alcohol-free. Before the website, orders came in through Instagram messages.

What it needed

Customers needed to see what Zarin makes and what it costs, choose their treats, and know the deposit, notice and delivery rules up front. Zarin needed prices that cover her supplies and pay her for her time.

What I did

Zarin hired me for this project. I designed and built the entire website, wrote the copy, created the illustrated character and animated hero, researched and wrote her pricing guide, and launched the site on her own domain.

What it includes

  • Five pages: Home, Menu & Bundles, Meet Zarin, Inquire, Book & FAQ
  • Full menu with prices by the dozen, add-ons and party bundles
  • Order builder: pick a date, pickup or delivery, treats and flavors, and see the estimated total as you go
  • Inquiry form for bigger events, with guest count and budget range
  • Delivery, notice and 50% deposit rules shown before anyone orders
  • An illustrated portrait of Zarin and an animated hero on the home page
  • Occasion section and a four-step "how ordering works"
  • Instagram links and a reviews section ready for real reviews
  • Built for phones first, with search basics and a sitemap
  • An eight-page dessert pricing guide based on her supply costs and 42 local listings
